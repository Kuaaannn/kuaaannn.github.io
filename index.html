About

Undergraduate student at the University of Warwick, studying Mathematics and Statistics. Research with Prof. Fanghui Liu on parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) for large language models and with Dr. Paris Giampouras in the RAISE Lab on post-training of large language models.

Research interests include pre-training and post-training of large language models, especially optimizers, parameter-efficient fine-tuning, and efficient training.
